Sources (for you, not for posting):
- Propaganda Movement aims: assimilation of the Philippines as a province of Spain, equal rights with Spaniards, representation in the Cortes, freedom of speech/press, equality before the law: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propaganda_Movement https://www.britannica.com/event/Propaganda-Movement
- Representation in the Cortes granted under the 1812 Constitution and retracted in 1837 (the movement wanted it back; see the "Aims" list): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propaganda_Movement
- La Solidaridad, founded 15 Feb 1889 in Barcelona (later Madrid): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Solidaridad
- Noli Me Tangere (1887), El Filibusterismo (1891): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Rizal
- "Filipinas dentro de cien años" (La Solidaridad, 1889-1890): Rizal warns the colony may break away if Spain refuses reform: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipinas_dentro_de_cien_a%C3%B1os

Notes on accuracy (kept on purpose):
- The video says assimilation was part of Rizal's EARLIER aims and the reformists' platform. It does not claim he formally renounced it or formally demanded independence.
- "Hihiwalay ang Pilipinas" is presented as a warning/prediction in the essay, not as a call for revolution.
